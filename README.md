# >_LEDGER

*EVE can be exchanged for goods and services.*

An order-book market for EVE Frontier storage units: anyone can post buy and sell orders, paid in EVE.

- Sell orders: items are held by the market until sold or cancelled.
- Buy orders: your EVE is held until the order fills or you cancel it.
- The unit's owner can set a fee (0 to 10%) on sales, pause the market, or hand it over.
- Contract: `0xc23061f99b6378debe88daaa18d51fec2937908b8bad4b4b0fd60cf298bbdad5` (module `exchange`), testnet Stillness.

This repo is the built site (GitHub Pages, main root). LUX is the in-game currency and is not an on-chain coin, so prices are in EVE only.
